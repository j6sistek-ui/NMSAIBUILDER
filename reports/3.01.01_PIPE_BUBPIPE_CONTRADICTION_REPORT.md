## Verdict

The new user-provided in-game/Blender comparison contradicts one 3.01.00 interpretation: clean Blender extraction data is not sufficient to classify BUBPIPE-family connected pipe behavior as verified.

## Contradictions found

| ObjectID / family | 3.01.00 interpretation | 3.01.01 correction |
|---|---|---|
| `PIPE` | zero-bbox review / caution | valid in-game pipe object, but Blender extraction is unreliable; require game/JSON validation before use |
| `BASE_BUBPIPE*` | `VERIFIED_DATA_OK` from clean bbox/rotation extraction | `GAME_VALIDATION_REQUIRED_PIPE_FAMILY`; Blender appearance/orientation differs from in-game result |
| connected pipe assemblies | component extraction implies mechanical confidence | RX90 may help individual pieces but does not validate bends, connection continuity, or local/global transform behavior |

## Non-contradictions

- The raw extraction records remain valid as raw Blender measurement data.
- The verified part-map overlay remains preferred for ordinary component mechanics outside documented exception families.
- The recipe-first assembly rule remains intact: assemblies require recipe/JSON/in-game validation when component mechanics are insufficient.

Use `rules/PIPE_BUBPIPE_VALIDATION_EXCEPTION_RULE.md` for all generated pipe runs or pipe-family part usage.
