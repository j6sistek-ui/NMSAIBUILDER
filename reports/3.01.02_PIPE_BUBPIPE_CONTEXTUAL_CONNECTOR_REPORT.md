# 3.01.02 PIPE/BUBPIPE Contextual Connector Evidence Report

## Verdict

The new exported JSON and in-game screenshots refine the 3.01.01 exception. PIPE/BUBPIPE are not merely bad Blender measurements; they are contextual connector parts where exported JSON transform data can be meaningful even when Blender static visuals/bbox are misleading.

## New evidence

- Exported JSON includes 16 `^BASE_BUBPIPE` placements and 3 `^PIPE` placements.
- The repeated BUBPIPE transforms share a stable local basis.
- Position deltas show approximately 2.0-unit steps along local Up, local At, and derived Right axes.
- In-game screenshots show a resolved translucent pipe network with bends/continuous runs.
- Blender screenshots show misleading column/disconnected-cylinder appearance and/or invisible `PIPE` behavior.

## Contradiction corrected

| Prior interpretation | 3.01.02 correction |
|---|---|
| PIPE/BUBPIPE Blender extraction is simply wrong/unusable. | Blender visual/bbox evidence is not authoritative, but JSON transforms are useful. |
| RX90 may fix the family. | RX90 may fix individual display/orientation, but does not validate connected assemblies. |
| Pipe parts can be evaluated like isolated component meshes. | Pipe parts must be evaluated as a local-axis connector graph / assembly recipe. |

## Operational rule

Use `rules/PROHIBITED_AND_PLACEHOLDER_OBJECTS.md` for all pipe-family parts and connected pipe assemblies.

## Build impact

Future high-quality builds may use pipe systems, but they must be marked as validation-required until confirmed by in-game screenshot/exported JSON/focused pipe harness. Pipe runs should be generated as node/edge graphs with approximately 2.0-unit local-axis steps, not as ordinary mesh-aligned cylinders.
