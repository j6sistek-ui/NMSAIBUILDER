# Single Master Changelog Governance Rule (4.01.00)

## Rule

There is one active root changelog: `CHANGELOG.md`. It contains the complete version history in reverse chronological order.

## Release notes and reports

Release-specific reports may exist under `reports/` and release notes may exist under `release/`, but they do not replace the master changelog. The source-package root must not accumulate release-specific changelog/open-topic files.

## Open topics

There is one active root open topics file: `OPEN_TOPICS_LOG.md`. Legacy open-topic files are moved under `archive/open_topics_legacy/` or consolidated.
